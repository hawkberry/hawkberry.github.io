FAQ

Are donations required?

No. All projects remain open source and freely available. Donations and sponsorship simply help support continued development and maintenance.

Does support include consulting?

No. Donations and sponsorship help fund open-source development and do not include consulting services. If you're looking for consulting related to a project, please see that project's documentation for consulting information.
